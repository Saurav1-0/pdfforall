About Us

A fast, browser-first PDF toolkit built with privacy as the default foundation.

Our Philosophy

Most online PDF converters silently upload sensitive personal documents, bank statements, contracts, and IDs to untrusted third-party cloud servers. PDFforAll was engineered from the ground up to execute all 27 PDF manipulations entirely inside your browser sandbox using modern WebAssembly and JavaScript engines.

Why Client-Side Matters

Your documents never traverse any backend networks or stay stored on external hard drives. Once you close your browser tab, your data remains solely yours.
