Privacy Policy

How PDFforAll handles your files and website information.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDFforAll.in (“PDFforAll”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit and use this website.

1. PDF files and local processing

Many PDFforAll tools are designed to process files locally in your browser. For those operations, the selected file is read by your browser and the resulting file is generated on your device; the file itself is not intentionally uploaded to a PDFforAll processing server. Some tools may load scripts, libraries, fonts or other resources from third-party hosting/CDN providers. Processing behaviour can vary by tool and browser. Do not use the website for highly sensitive documents unless you are satisfied with the tool's behaviour and your own device security.

2. Information collected by the website

Our hosting provider and website infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, request time, browser information, requested URL and security logs. We do not ask you to create an account to use the current PDF tools.

3. Cookies and optional analytics

Essential browser storage may be used to remember your cookie choice or support website functionality. If configured, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are optional analytics services. Their scripts are loaded only after you select “Accept analytics” in the consent banner. If you reject optional analytics, those analytics scripts are not loaded by our consent manager. You can clear your browser's site storage to reset your choice.

Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, device/browser details, approximate location derived from network information, interaction events and session interaction data. We do not intentionally send PDF contents, document text or uploaded filenames to analytics services. Avoid entering personal or confidential information into page fields that may be recorded by analytics tools.

4. Third-party services

The website uses Vercel hosting and currently includes Vercel Web Analytics on most pages. Vercel may process website usage and technical information under its own privacy terms. If you choose to accept optional analytics and the IDs are configured, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity will also load. The website may additionally load fonts, PDF libraries and other resources from third-party content delivery providers.

5. Contact messages

If you contact us, we may use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry. Do not include passwords, payment details or confidential document contents in a message.

6. Data retention and security

We do not operate a PDF document storage account in the current website experience. Technical logs and any contact correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for security, support and operational purposes. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You can reject optional analytics using the consent banner. You can also clear site data in your browser. You may use browser privacy controls to limit cookies and tracking, although some site functionality may be affected.

8. Children

PDFforAll is a general-purpose utility website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please use our Contact page.
